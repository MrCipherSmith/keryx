### Findings

### [F-001] Device login await has no bound of its own (question)
- **Severity**: info
- **File**: src/remote/command-router.ts (connectAddable)
- **Hot path**: once per Telegram [Connect] press on a device-login provider; not a loop and not a per-request path.
- **Problem**: `outcome = await host.connectWithDeviceLogin(provider.id, notify);` has no timeout in the router. commandLimitMs does not cover it, because the picker flow runs detached.
- **Why it matters**: only if the host login never settles: `idle()` would stay pending and each extra press adds another pending flow. The host side is not in this slice, so this stays a question.
- **Fix**: confirm the host login expires with the device code, or race it against a limit.
