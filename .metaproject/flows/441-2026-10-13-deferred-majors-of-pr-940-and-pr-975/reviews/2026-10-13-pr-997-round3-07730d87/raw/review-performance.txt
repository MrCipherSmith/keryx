### Measured passes per call (bun, parseReviewResult from ledger.ts)
Passes over the reply: labelledFence (1 regex pass), MARKER matchAll (1), fenceRanges + fenceTester in afterMarker (2), objectEnd per kept marker (at most 64, each only to its own close, to the end when unclosed), topLevelObjects (1 pass, plus at most 65 rescans to the end for unclosed fragments), fenceRanges + fenceTester again in scannedResult (2). The fence work is built twice but each build is linear and costs 0.5-17 ms at 1 MB (many fences 16.9 ms, many inline triple backticks 6.2 ms, many labelled fences 2 ms). Not worth caching.
- Prose-only 1 MB: 0.9 ms. Real marker result after 1 MB prose: 0.8 ms.
- 1 MB, worst shapes: 64 unclosed prose braces 122 ms; 200 unclosed markers 232 ms; marker plus candidate unclosed 232 ms; dense unclosed 117 ms. 4 MB, 64 markers: 941 ms (4.0x for 4x size: linear, no quadratic term). 8 KB: 0.9-2.3 ms; 11 KB adversarial: 0.29 ms.
- Comment claim 'about 3 ms per MB': one unclosed scan over 1 MB measured 3.08 ms. Accurate for one scan; 64 scans are 122 ms.
- MARKER backtracking: no catastrophic case. The lookbehind limits attempts to line starts; the prefix class cannot cross a newline; the trailing whitespace-then-brace lookahead backs off once per marker. 1 MB of dashes on one line 1.5 ms; 1 MB of spaces after a marker 3.2 ms; 1 MB of 'REVIEW_RESULT   ' lines 1.5 ms; 1 MB of 'REVIEW_RESULT:' plus blank lines 1.9 ms.
