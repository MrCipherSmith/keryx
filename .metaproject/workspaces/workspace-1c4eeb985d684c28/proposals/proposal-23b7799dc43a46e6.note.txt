Review-surface gap to consider fixing: broken/unresolvable items show up as `unknown` with no reason. In src/sac/catch-up.ts a session is classified in this priority order — fresh lock held → excluded entirely; terminal-state.json → "blocked"; newest *-unbound-candidate.json → "unbound-candidate"; all-failed wrap-up outcome → "unknown" PLUS a wrapUpOutcome{trigger,generatedAt,groups}; otherwise any session with slate engagement → plain "unknown" with only lastSeenAt. So two very different situations (a wrap-up that genuinely failed vs. wrap-up that never ran) render as the same opaque item unless the outcome artifact happens to exist; the file's own comment admits this and says the artifact was added precisely to tell them apart. Two concrete improvements: (1) the review output should always name WHY an item is unknown (which of the four checks produced it, and whether a slate was engaged at all), and (2) a session with a malformed/unreadable slate or evidence file — a genuinely "broken" item — should get its own named category rather than collapsing into unknown (same reasoning that already keeps "unreviewed-sac-path" out of it).
