Two measurements worth not re-deriving. (1) DDG's anomaly window is long-lived once deeply tripped: still HTTP 202 at +2/+5/+10 minutes of quiet, so the retry ladder cannot and should not outlast it — the honest exhausted-ladder error is the right outcome, and probing DDG "to check" extends the window. (2) Disconfirmed hypothesis: the anomaly is NOT triggered by keryx's missing Accept-Encoding (a controlled A/B showed 202 both with `accept-encoding: gzip, deflate` and without); no header/compression work is warranted for that reason.
