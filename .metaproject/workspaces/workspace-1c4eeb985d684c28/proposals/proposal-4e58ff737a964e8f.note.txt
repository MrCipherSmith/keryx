Search-provider landscape worth capturing for the search subsystem page. Three families: vendor-hosted search (provider-side grounding, gated on the provider); bring-your-own-key (Exa, Perplexity, Brave, Tavily or Firecrawl behind a plugin or MCP server); and scraped DuckDuckGo Lite — keryx's approach. Another tool on that path detects the same 202 + three anomaly markers but does NOT retry, instead telling its model 'Do not retry or rephrase; wait a few minutes or fetch known URLs directly'. keryx adopted that wording, and sends a browser-shaped header set for public-search only.
