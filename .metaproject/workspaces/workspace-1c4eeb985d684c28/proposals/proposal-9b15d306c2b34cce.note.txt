The retry ladder is a mitigation, not a cure: under sustained burst use the window outlives both rungs and a search fails after ~25s. Durable answers for users who need many searches per turn: an API-key provider (Tavily/Exa/Brave — endpoints verified reachable from this host, 401/422/404 without a key) or a proxy/VPN egress. Also: the inter-search gap is per-process state, not a global lock, so several keryx clients (terminal + editor + another shell) hammer DDG independently and trip the limiter faster.
