### Findings

### [PC-W3-001] Desktop bridge bullet omits the new `include_closed` parameter and its refusal

- **Severity**: minor
- **File**: apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/review.rs:78
- **Problem**: PR body, What changes → Desktop bridge: "`review_board` gains `reviews_only`, which maps to `review reviews --json [--repo]` and is refused in combination with any other board mode." The delta adds a second bridge parameter: `paged: bool, cursor: Option<&str>, badges: Option<BadgeTarget>, reviews_only: bool, include_closed: bool)`, refused outside the Reviews list (`"configuration: Closed Reviews can be included only in the Reviews list"`), and appended as `--include-closed`; dev/reviewBoard.ts mirrors it (`if (args.includeClosed === true) argv.push("--include-closed");`). The CLI bullet says Desktop passes `--include-closed`, but the bridge bullet, which describes the mapping, still stops at `[--repo]`.
- **Why it matters**: a reader of the bridge contract (stage-1/3 author, CI reviewer of the unverified Rust) does not learn that `review_board` accepts `includeClosed`, or that it is an error outside `reviews_only`.
- **Fix**: "`review_board` gains `reviews_only` and `include_closed`, mapping to `review reviews --json [--repo] [--include-closed]`; `reviews_only` is refused with any other board mode, `include_closed` without `reviews_only`."

### [PC-W3-002] Expected-overlap guidance for ReviewBoard.tsx / App.tsx predates the fix round, on lines stage 1 also edits

- **Severity**: minor
- **File**: apps/desktop/src/review/ReviewBoard.tsx:527
- **Problem**: PR body, Expected overlap: "`ReviewBoard.tsx`: stage 2 adds the `onOpenInReviews` prop, routes `openReview` view/answer to it, removes `ProjectReviewRules` from the toolbar, and adds `outdated.round` to `badgesEqual`" and "`App.tsx` ...: the new tab and the D4 shortcut order". The delta further changes ReviewBoard.tsx: removes the `onOpenReviewSession` prop (`-  onOpenReviewSession?: (key: DeskRunKey, run: DeskRun) => Promise<void>;`) and its pass-through to ReviewDetail (`-      reviewModels={reviewModels} {...(onOpenReviewSession && { onOpenReviewSession })}`), whose prop is also deleted from ReviewDetail.tsx; changes `onOpenInReviews` to `(target: { repo: string; repositoryId: string; number: number }, intent?)`; adds `onWatcherPoll` and `onIdentityChange`. App.tsx gains `reviewsFocusHandled`, `noteWatcherPoll`, `boardIdentity` and new `<ReviewBoard>` props. origin/feat/desktop-review-v2-stage1 edits exactly these lines: it rewrites the `function RepositoryBoard({ ... onOpenReviewSession, ...` destructuring, keeps `{...(onOpenReviewSession && { onOpenReviewSession })}` on the ReviewDetail element, and rewrites the `<ReviewBoard ...>` element in App.tsx.
- **Why it matters**: the overlap section is the merge guide ("Keep both" / "Take stage 1's ..."). Following it, a merger resolving to stage 1's ReviewBoard lines drops the repo-carrying target, the watcher-poll and identity props (the round-1 multi-repo and hidden-indicator fixes) or re-adds the removed `onOpenReviewSession` pass-through; the type check catches the last, not the first two if the props stay optional.
- **Fix**: extend the ReviewBoard.tsx and App.tsx overlap entries with the fix-round changes: `onOpenReviewSession` removed from ReviewBoard and ReviewDetail; `onOpenInReviews` takes `{repo, repositoryId, number}`; new `onWatcherPoll` and `onIdentityChange` (App.tsx wires them to the Reviews tab with `onFocusHandled`, `reloadSignal`, `rulesTarget`) — keep them when taking stage 1's board.
