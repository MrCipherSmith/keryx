### [F-001] The plan presents the migration check as verification, but that check ran before D-R1 and FA-11 changed the migration rule
- **Severity**: minor
- **File**: docs/requirements/keryx-desktop-review-v2/implementation-plan.md (section «Состояние этапа 2», the paragraph «Миграция проверена на копии рабочей базы …»)
- **Problem**: The delta rewrites the «Хранилище» bullet to the new rule. Under D-R1, a range that returns after others (A, B, A) opens a new round. Under FA-11, a request superseded before it started keeps no round, and the next request takes its number. The paragraph just below still states, unchanged, that the migration was verified on a copy of the working database: «раундов 1 — 13, раундов 2 — 4». The journal records that check as T9 (journal.md:31-34). That entry is dated before review round 1 (journal.md:106-110), which is where D-R1 and FA-11 were decided and implemented (85fa985c1). I found no journal entry that re-ran the copy check after the fix round. Under the new rule, the same store can number its rounds differently, either through an A,B,A sequence or through a superseded queued row.
- **Why it matters**: A reader of «Что не проверено» and of the verification paragraph takes the 13/4 numbers as evidence for the rule the plan describes. They are evidence for the old rule. Agent conduct rule 5 asks that what was not verified be named. The plan's «Проверка» section also lists "тест миграции на копии рабочей базы" as the stage-2 check.
- **Fix**: Re-run the T9 copy check at head and update the numbers. Or mark the paragraph as measured before D-R1/FA-11, and add "the migration under the D-R1 rule was not re-checked on a copy of the working database" to «Что не проверено».
- **Evidence**: `git diff 20e4281a2..f6971fd4a -- docs/requirements/keryx-desktop-review-v2/implementation-plan.md` changes the «Хранилище» bullet and leaves the «Миграция проверена» line untouched. `keryx ctx rg "runs.sqlite|D-R1|superseded"` over journal.md and fix-round-1.md finds the T9 copy check only at journal.md:31-34; D-R1 is at journal.md:110 and fix-round-1.md:7; FA-11 is at fix-round-1.md:24.
