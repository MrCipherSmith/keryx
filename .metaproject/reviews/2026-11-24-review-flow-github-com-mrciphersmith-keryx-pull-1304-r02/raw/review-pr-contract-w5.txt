### Findings

### [PC-W5-001] specification.md's state table and freshness rule still derive everything from the last attempt; the delta excludes archived attempts, and the plan and cli-reference say so

- **Severity**: minor
- **File**: docs/requirements/keryx-desktop-review-v2/specification.md:65
- **Problem**: specification.md:57 "Состояние выводится из последнего раунда и публикаций", :61-67 every row keyed on "последний Attempt" / "последний раунд", and :70 freshness "head последнего проверенного раунда". AC4 says "state and freshness follow the specification table". The delta (slice-05 :780-784, :833-855) adds `stateAttempts(row)`: "the unarchived ones, or all when every one is archived", and state, latestRound, reviewed, findings, publication, flowId, tokens and the freshness head (`referenceHead`) all come from that set. The test "archived attempts stay listed and flagged, but state, findings, publication and freshness ignore them" (slice-05 :1141-1151) pins a Review whose newest attempt (round 2, archived, published, reviewed head c) yields `state: "ready"`, `latestRound: 1`, `freshness: current` against head b. Under the spec table, the last attempt/round is round 2, published, and freshness compares head c. implementation-plan.md:36 ("Архивные попытки остаются в раундах с пометкой `archived` и не влияют на состояние") and cli-reference.md:5205 state the exception; the spec does not, and it is not among the plan's "Решения, принятые на этапе" (:43-46), which do list the two other deviations from the same table (budget, failed retry).
- **Why it matters**: The spec is the document AC4 cites. Stage 3/4 authors and the stage-1 card link (which reads `keryx review reviews`) will derive state from the newest attempt and get a different state, round and freshness than the CLI returns for a Review with an archived latest attempt.
- **Fix**: Add the archived rule to the state section (and the freshness sentence) of specification.md, and list it under the plan's stage-2 decisions. Same class as PC-W4-002 (spec :75 omits plan-only rules): the plan's other stage-2 exception on that line, history imported later receiving the next round regardless of its older `createdAt` (implementation-plan.md:35; desk-run-store.test.ts "history imported later is numbered when it is recorded ... (known limitation)", slice-05 :949-956), is also missing from spec :75 "раунды идут в порядке `createdAt`"; fix both on that line together.

### [PC-W5-002] cli-reference.md describes `reviewed` as the newest finished reviewed attempt and lists only state, findings and publication as ignoring archived attempts

- **Severity**: minor
- **File**: docs/docs/cli-reference.md:5197
- **Problem**: cli-reference.md:5197 "the range its newest finished reviewed attempt covered (`reviewed`, or `null`)" and :5205-5206 "Archived attempts stay in the rounds with `archived: true` and do not decide the state, findings or publication". The delta computes `reviewed` from `reference = newest(attempts, reviewedResult)` over `attempts = stateAttempts(row)` (slice-05 :833-852), and `referenceHead` (freshness) from the same unarchived set (:786-791). The test at slice-05 :1141-1151 expects `reviewed: { baseSha: sha("a"), headSha: sha("b") }` and `freshness: { kind: "current" }` while the newest finished reviewed attempt is the archived one with head c. `latestRound` also comes from the unarchived set (`latestRound: 1` there). The ReviewSummary doc comment (review-entity.ts, slice-05 :747) repeats the same unqualified wording.
- **Why it matters**: A script consumer reading the CLI reference takes `reviewed`, freshness and `latestRound` to cover archived attempts, and gets a different range and round than the JSON carries when the newest attempt is archived.
- **Fix**: Extend the sentence: archived attempts do not decide the state, `latestRound`, `reviewed`, freshness, findings or publication (unless every attempt is archived); say "newest finished, unarchived reviewed attempt" at :5197 and in the ReviewSummary comment.
