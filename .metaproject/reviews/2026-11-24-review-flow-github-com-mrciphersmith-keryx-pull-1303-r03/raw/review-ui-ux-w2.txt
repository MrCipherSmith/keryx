## Summary (review-ui-ux, wave 2, round 3, slice-02)

**Verdict:** DONE. No blocker or major findings; 1 minor and 1 info.

**Context**
- Product: Keryx Desktop's Publish to GitHub dialog, plus the review text it posts to GitHub. App screen.
- Audience: the operator publishing a local review.
- Primary task: publish the review, or learn why it cannot be published and what to do instead.
- Mode: code only, with no URL or screenshots. The surface in this slice is copy: the `blockedReason` strings, the as-is body line, thrown messages and the docs.

**Holds (verification)**
- The round-1 inline/General remedy is fixed. With the head moved, the reason no longer suggests inline comments.
- The as-is body line says which commit was reviewed, where the PR moved, and that the newer commits are not covered. The docs describe the same thing.
- The docs match the dialog labels, the dropped automatic publication, carry-over of answers by finding id, and the reuse of an existing attempt. Round 2 UX-105 (closed by wave 1) is confirmed from this slice's docs hunk.
- Automatic publication on a moved head fails with "This review needs a publication decision in Desktop.", and no as-is text is ever auto-posted.
- The superseded message cites a round number the Reviews panel displays.

**Findings**
- **UX-301 (minor).** "Round N of this review is newer. Publish from that round." Per the recorded decision, it also blocks for a failed, running, deleted or archived newer round, where that remedy cannot be followed. Only the copy is filed, not the block.
- **UX-302 (info).** The generic "closed or its revision moved" reason now also covers a transient compare failure, with no refresh hint, and a rewritten history, with no next step. The docs omit the superseded-round refusal.

**Not re-filed:** wave 1's UX-201 and UX-202, the decided UX-101 and UX-103, and the open UX-104. Carried answers are still unmarked, and earlier-round carry-over now also pre-selects answers, which is the same info.
