## Findings

### [PC-001] "Attempt of any state" — the Update guard skips archived and imported attempts
- **Severity**: minor
- **File**: apps/desktop/src/review/ReviewWorkspace.tsx:135
- **Problem**: Body: "If the current head already has an attempt of any state, that attempt is shown instead of starting a second one". Code: `const covering = local.runs.filter(run => run.source !== "existing" && !reviewIsArchived(run) && sameRange(run, revision));` — an archived attempt (`decision.action === "archive"`) or an imported (`existing`) attempt on the current head does not stop a new start.
- **Why it matters**: the owner reviewing the "Decisions taken in the flow" list reads that no second attempt can ever exist on the target head; with an archived one it can.
- **Fix**: correct the claim ("an attempt in any state that is not archived or imported"), or drop the two filters if "any state" is the intended decision.
