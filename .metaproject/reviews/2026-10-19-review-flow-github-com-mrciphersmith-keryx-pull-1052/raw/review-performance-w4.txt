## Performance Review (slice-04, src/tui/*)

### Checked, no finding
- Disk reads or scans of flows/reviews in the render path. src/tui/operator-confirmations.ts imports only REMOTE_CARD_TTL_MS and does no fs, no store read and no flow/review scan. Every function (pendingConfirmationLine, answeredConfirmationLine, confirmationLines, waitingCardsText) is a pure function over the in-memory log. OperatorConfirmationLog.waiting() and entries() are 1 to 3 array filters over (unanswered + at most 5 answered) entries.
- decisions-surface.ts. openDecisions awaits reportText(cwd) as before (one journal read per modal open, unchanged by the PR) and then calls confirmationLines?.() once, so the cards are an in-memory snapshot at open time. withConfirmationLines is a string join. The keypress handler (scroll and repaint over the precomputed rows) is untouched, and openDecisionsArms drops the new option. The sidebar poller (refresh, count, backfilled) is untouched; options.confirmationLines is only forwarded into openWith.
- Per-keystroke work. None added. The new operatorCards.subscribe listener fires only on ask() and answer(), meaning a card put to the operator or answered, once per event. It calls liveRemotePanel.refresh() (a handful of text-node assignments) plus the open /remote-control modal listeners.
- Sidebar draw. getWaitingCards runs waiting(), an O(n) filter over under 10 entries, per draw. Draws are already driven by the bridge change notices.
- Unbounded lists. Answered entries are capped at ANSWERED_CARDS_KEPT = 5. Waiting entries grow only with human-scale operator cards (see F-001 for the leak edge).
- Import cost. operator-confirmations.ts imports operator-confirm-tools only for the REMOTE_CARD_TTL_MS constant. That module is already imported by src/tui/tui-shell.ts, src/commands/agent.ts and interactive-agent-tools.ts, so no new module is loaded into the TUI.
- approval-mirror.ts. onUndelivered is an optional callback invoked at most once per topic answer, and no extra timers or listeners are added. Pre-existing and unchanged: AbortSignal.any per race.
- mirrorUndeliveredNote (src/remote/shell-bridge.ts:296, outside the slice but read) reads two booleans. isCard and the note are computed once per approval, not in a loop.
- remoteApproval() allocates one Date per approval. Negligible.
- Synchronous blocking, bundle imports and redundant API calls: no new fs sync calls, no new heavy imports and no new network calls in the slice.

### Not findings (noted)
- The age and "time left of the 1-hour token" text is computed with Date.now() at repaint only, so an open modal shows stale ages until the next change or keypress. This is a display-freshness point, not a performance one, and it is left to the logic reviewer.
