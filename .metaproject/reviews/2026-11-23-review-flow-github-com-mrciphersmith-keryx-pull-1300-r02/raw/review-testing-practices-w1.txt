## Findings

### [TP-R2-001] App fixtures synchronise on a fixed 40 ms delay
- Severity: major, from tdd-workflow.mdc Review severities: "Sleep or fixed delay for async work (Rule 6) | always | major".
- File: apps/desktop/src/test/app-work-row.fixture.tsx (added by a34d8961b). Sibling: apps/desktop/src/test/app-navigation.fixture.tsx, added in this PR by 21030edf0 (not on base b147408a8).
- Problem: `settle()` waits a fixed 40 ms inside act. It is the only thing that orders the asserts after `session/new`, the flow-list refresh on `focus` and the `_keryx/session_flags` write. `packagesAppear` uses two settles. Afterwards the asserts check `flagWrites`, the composer value, and the negatives "Nothing more is linked" and `slot(13, "flow") === "Create Flow"`.
- Why it matters: on a loaded CI runner the refresh can land after 80 ms. The positive asserts then fail at random. The negative asserts after `packagesAppear("507-a", "508-b")` pass whether or not the refreshed list was ever seen. Locally R2-07 is red on this fixture, so the path does run here, but nothing in the fixture proves that it ran.
- Fix: replace `settle` with a `waitFor(predicate, deadline)` like the composer-ready loop already in the file. Wait for `flagWrites.some(w => w.flow)` or for the slot text. For the "two link none" case, first wait for a positive artifact that the new list arrived, for example a `flows_list` call count after the focus event or the Flows tab showing 507/508, and only then assert that nothing was written.
