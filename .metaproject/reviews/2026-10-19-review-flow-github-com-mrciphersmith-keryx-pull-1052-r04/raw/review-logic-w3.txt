Finding
F-001 (major): review_complete cannot record dispositions.
completeSpec (operator-confirm-review.ts:700) takes only `package` (inputSchema at :707), and apply calls completeReviewGated (:756) without `dispositions`. recordDispositions is reachable only through completeManagedReview options, whose only caller is runComplete, which reads --finding/--disposition/--evidence. The review-orchestrator skill (SKILL.md:247, 284-297) still tells the operator flow to pass those flags, says an agent never runs `keryx review complete` (:136), and says closing a fix round without dispositions leaves every finding `unknown`. Through the card, a fix round therefore always closes with every prior finding `unknown`.
