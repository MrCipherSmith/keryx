## Findings

### F-001 cli-reference says plain `review complete` needs a terminal; it does not
- Severity: major (actively wrong statement)
- File: docs/docs/cli-reference.md:4921
- The added sentence lists `complete` among "the operator-only steps here (`confirm-plan`, `decide-premise`, `chunks estimate --answer`, `complete`, ...) need a terminal". In src/commands/review.ts (complete handler, about lines 4554-4562) the TTY check applies only to `--waive-coverage` and `--waive`; plain `complete` has none. TM-04 section 8, in the same diff, says so ("A plain `review complete` has no TTY check"). The two docs contradict each other, and the CLI reference overstates a guard that does not exist.
- Fix: write "`complete --waive-coverage`, `complete --waive`" in the terminal-only list and add that plain `complete` has no terminal check (the card rule binds the agent, as TM-04 section 8 says).

### F-002 limitations.md: parenthetical contradicts the sentence it sits in
- Severity: minor
- File: docs/docs/limitations.md:187
- The sentence reads "A Telegram or web card for these approvals (the review acts ... already have one in a shell driven from a topic: a Telegram press proves ... see decision TM-04), session-wide grants from a remote answer, and approvals for unattended trigger runs are not built." The 400-character parenthetical splits subject and predicate and says the card exists inside a sentence that ends "are not built". It is hard to read and easy to misread.
- Fix: leave the original sentence as it was and move the five-tool note into the existing parenthetical about `/remote-control` a few lines below, or into its own sentence.

### F-003 TM-04 count of decisions does not match its own table
- Severity: info
- File: docs/decisions/keryx-harness/TM-04-operator-confirmation-cards.md:15
- "Nineteen keryx decisions" (was "Thirteen") while section 2 lists 17 tool rows at head (14 at the base, so the base count was already one off). The diff adds three tools and three decision kinds, which is not +6.
- Fix: count from the table and say "Seventeen" (or drop the number).

### F-004 Same long paragraph pasted twice; new prose breaks the file's hard wrap
- Severity: info
- Files: docs/docs/guides/review-with-a-record.md:603 and :734 (also .metaproject/skills/gdskills/review/review-premise/SKILL.md:99)
- The clause "an allowed Telegram user only, for that card, once, within one hour; the record keeps `confirmedVia: remote-card` with `pressedBy` and `pressedAt`). If the topic cannot be reached the card says the Telegram mirror was not delivered and you answer it in the shell" appears word for word at 603 and 734 (about 500 characters each), and again in cli-reference and TM-04 6a. The surrounding text in these two files is hard-wrapped near 80 columns; the new lines are single 400-500 character lines.
- Fix: state the rule once in the guide (the premise section) and have the waiver section point to it, as line 967 already does with "see above"; wrap the new lines like their neighbours.
