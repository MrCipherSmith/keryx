### Findings

#### [AR-W1-001] Two skill sentences still send the agent to `keryx review complete` after a waiver card
- Severity: minor
- File: .metaproject/skills/gdskills/review/review-orchestrator/SKILL.md (line 782) and SKILL.detail.md (line 386); identical text in src/gdskills/bundled/skills/review/review-orchestrator/
- Problem: The delta rewrites the "Done when" bullet, the "Operator decisions" bullet, "Round plan" step 6 and the readiness section to say an agent closes a round only through the `review_complete` card and never runs `keryx review complete`. SKILL.md line 782 (chunk coverage) still says "in keryx shell call `review_waive_coverage` (a card, never a terminal), then run `keryx review complete`", and SKILL.detail.md line 386 (readiness item 10) says "in keryx shell call `review_waive_readiness` ..., then run `keryx review complete <package>`". The same file's "Done when" says the opposite.
- Why it matters: this is the D-01 shape in the skill layer. TM-04 section 8 says plain `review complete` has no TTY check, so the card is a rule for the agent and the skills are the only place that rule is stated. An agent that follows line 782 or 386 after a waiver closes the round with no `review_complete` card, no `confirmedVia` and no `pressedBy`. The two lines are not in the diff, so the contradiction is new with this change.
- Fix: change both to "then call the `review_complete` card" (keep the CLI wording only for "elsewhere"), in the .metaproject copy and the bundled copy (4 files). Same pass: SKILL.md "Round plan" step 9 ends the tail with "`readiness`, `complete`" and "Early verdict" says "`keryx review complete` continue as the tail"; name the card there too.
- Confidence: medium (the lines are untouched by the PR; whether they count as in scope is the panel's call, hence minor)
