### Every in-flight request that reads a counter or the flag

- Catalog read (any kind): reads R only. Retired exactly when a later read starts, on dependency change, or on unmount. Never left applying after something newer.
- Wiki-link lookup (`openPath`): reads P (plus token, selection, mode). Retired by Refresh, Retry, a rebuild reload, a dependency change, unmount; not retired by a quiet read. Too early: no. Never: no, since a non-quiet read always bumps P.
- Page of results (`loadMoreSearch`): reads P and `latestSearch.generation`. Same as above; additionally retired when a quiet read replaces the catalog snapshot. Pagination is `pageInFlight`-guarded and cleared in `finally` on every path.
- Visible effect: reads Pend and `loaded`. Pend is cleared on completion of the current generation and re-set by every body, so it cannot be left true after the last read of an instance (a superseded read never clears it, but its superseder always sets and later clears it). The one way it stays true is a read that never settles; the Rust side bounds that at 150 s.
