## Summary

Slice-10 second look (security). I found 1 major, 1 minor and 1 info.

- **SEC-R2-001 (major)** — `ledger.ts` `screenedAs`: a raw finding is closed `out-of-scope` whenever a screened entry has the same reviewer and bare display id. Waves reuse display ids, so a finding the orchestrator left out of findings.json can be closed by another wave's scope-B screen. The ledger then reconciles with no gap.
- **SEC-R2-002 (minor)** — `locate.ts`: the substring pass has no minimum quote length, so a one-token fragment produces a `derived` anchor.
- **SEC-R2-003 (info)** — `completion-gate.ts`: the session-name tracker trusts start-of-line output from any tool call whose arguments mention `keryx review`, and it matches packages with no reviewId by directory name. The impact is bounded to cwd: a misdirected nudge at most.
