- **NEW-TP5-003 (recordSaved guard).** The recordSaved guard's skip branch is not exercised by the test that names it.

On `expectLinearTime`: the best-of-three runs per size and the 12x-plus-25 ms bound keep flake risk low. The same slack makes it blind to small super-linear regressions, and the deterministic exec-spy checks were removed.
