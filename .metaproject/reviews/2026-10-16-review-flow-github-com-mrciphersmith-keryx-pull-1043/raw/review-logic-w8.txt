- Spec compliance gate: ran against flows/459 description (AC1/AC2 machinery present in this slice; no criterion is missing from it)

### Summary
mergePremises keeps its invariant: it adds nothing, never raises severity, cancels duplicate claims, rejects self-judgement, and stops downgrades at minor. The defects sit where readers meet. The held-finding lookup and the interim verdict read the raw claims file, while readiness and the flow gate read only findings.json. As a result the operator can answer a question whose answer changes nothing, and a stale claims file holds a finding in the verdict but not at the gate (LG8-001). Smaller gaps: claims that name a duplicate the ingest merge absorbed are lost (LG8-002), a carried premise is lost when its finding is absorbed (LG8-003), readiness ignores dispositions that the verdict honours (LG8-004), and the cap can drop a finding that a downgrade made cap-eligible (LG8-005).

### Logic Bugs
- [LG8-001] major: findHeldFinding and the verdict treat raw premises.json claims as holds; readiness and the gate do not.
- [LG8-002] minor: a claim naming an absorbed duplicate is rejected as `unknown-finding`; `merged_from` is not consulted.
- [LG8-003] minor: a carried `upheld` premise is dropped when its finding is absorbed into a canonical with no premise.
- [LG8-005] minor: a premise downgrade (or a hold) can make a finding cap-eligible and drop it from findings.json.
