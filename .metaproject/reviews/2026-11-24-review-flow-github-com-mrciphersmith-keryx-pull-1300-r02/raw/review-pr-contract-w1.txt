## Findings

### [PC-009] Lock refusal documented as the outcome of a long hold; the 2 s stale takeover comes first
- Severity: minor
- File: docs/docs/cli-reference.md (also implementation-plan.md:35, PR body Choices)
- Problem: cli-reference.md (added in this range): "when another keryx process holds it too long the request is refused with a message ending in "retry"". implementation-plan.md:35: "параллельный писатель ждёт до 3 секунд, затем получает ошибку «retry»". PR body: "A flags write waits up to 3 s for the lock, then fails with 'retry'." Code: FLAGS_LOCK_TIMING is `{ timeoutMs: 3_000, staleMs: 2_000 }`; LockAttempt.step checks the deadline, then removes any lock whose mtime is older than staleMs and retries. A waiter that starts at t0 sees the holder's lock go stale at or before t0+2 s, removes it and takes it before the t0+3 s deadline. A live holder that holds too long is therefore broken, not waited out, and the waiter's write proceeds. The "retry" refusal fires only when the stale lock cannot be removed (unwritable dir) or other writers keep winning the retake. The tests confirm it: every refusal test passes `staleMs: 60_000` with a short timeout (organize.test.ts:234, :300).
- Impact: a client author or owner reading the reference or the "Choices" list expects concurrent writers to be serialized, with a retryable refusal after 3 s. In fact a second writer goes ahead after about 2 s while the first may still be inside its critical section (the risk HL3-004/SC3-001 defer). The owner is asked to accept "waits up to 3 s, then fails", which is not what production timing does.
- Fix: describe the real rule in all three places: "a lock older than 2 s is taken as abandoned; the request is refused with 'retry' only when the lock cannot be taken within 3 s (e.g. it cannot be removed)". Or set staleMs ≥ timeoutMs if the documented behaviour is the intended one (a logic-lane decision, tied to HL3-004).
