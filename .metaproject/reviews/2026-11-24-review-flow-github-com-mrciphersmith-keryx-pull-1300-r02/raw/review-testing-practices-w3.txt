## Isolation, cleanup, determinism (executed)

- **HOME isolation.** I ran the 4 slice suites (organize, issue-session, file-lock, issues-board; 54 pass) with HOME and TMPDIR pointed at empty scratch dirs. Nothing was written under HOME except bun's own cache. The spawned `bun -e` writers inherit the test env and wrote nothing outside TMPDIR either.
- **Temp dirs.** file-lock.test.ts and issue-session.test.ts remove every root in `finally`. organize.test.ts leaves its 20 project() roots and the 3 new barrier dirs behind. That is TP-R4-005, already filed, so it is not re-filed here.
- **Child processes.** No child is ever killed: there is no `child.kill()`. Each barrier test still writes `go` in a `finally`, so every child is released on every parent path, including when an assertion inside `try` fails or `until()` throws. `until()` stops waiting when its child exits. After all runs, no `bun -e` process was left. I found no path that orphans a child short of killing the parent test process.
- **Determinism under load.** I ran organize + file-lock + issue-session as 8 parallel processes, 3 times over (24 runs). Result: 24/24 green, 39 pass each. That includes the 200-round two-process test with the production 3 s / 2 s timing, and the staleMs 1 takeover test.
- **Producer messages in issues-board.test.ts.** The new tests import the producer constant `issuesDisabled` from issues-github.ts:10, or spell the producer text verbatim (`unusableRemote` at issues-board.ts:149, `invalidInput` at issues-github.ts:8). `rejects.toThrow(issuesDisabled)` is a real distinction: the repository-named 410 text (issues-board.ts:188) does not contain the generic one. The viewer-page-of-PRs fixture uses GitHub's `link: <…?page=N>; rel="next"` shape.
- **First-bind error (probe).** "the first bind fails loudly" uses a bare `.rejects.toThrow()`. I logged the error, and it is the intended one: `EISDIR … rename … flags.json.<pid>.<uuid>.tmp -> …/flags.json`, raised by the subject write. The test pins the right path, so this is not a finding.
- **Spy test.** `spyOn(organize, "backfillSessionSubject")` calls through and is restored in `finally`, and wave 1's B08 shows it intercepts. However, the test's title claims "reads the flags without the lock", and that claim is not pinned (TP-R4-011).
