## Findings

### [F-001] The worker's hand-off of `previousPartial` is unpinned, and the only test of the worker rule is a source-text assertion
- Severity: minor
- File: src/review/desk-run-service.ts:399 (`previousPartial: previous.partial`); src/review/desk-run-service.test.ts:78-83
- Problem: M27 (`previousPartial: false`) leaves service, executor and commands/review-desk-run suites green, 48/48. No test runs `runDeskWorker` at all; a search over `src/**/*.test.ts` for `runDeskWorker` finds no matches. The test "the worker has one previous-attempt rule" reads desk-run-service.ts as text and asserts substrings, so it pins spelling rather than behaviour. It does not see what reaches `executeDeskReview`.
- Why it matters: AC7 depends on this one field. With it lost, every partial previous attempt is silently not handed on (executor M20 proves the executor obeys the flag). The next round becomes a full review, and every suite stays green.
- Fix: extract the options assembly (`previousRunDir`/`previousPartial` from `previousRoundAttempt` + `store.directory`) into a pure function and unit-test it on a partial and a completed prior attempt. Or drive `runDeskWorker` through a stubbed `executeDeskReview` port and assert the options it receives. Then drop the `readFileSync(desk-run-service.ts)` substring test.

### [F-002] The same-Review gate on the answer carry-over chain is unpinned
- Severity: minor
- File: src/review/desk-publication.ts:130-142 (`sameReview`, `earlierAttemptDirectories`)
- Problem: M04 (reviewId clause -> true) and M05 (drop `!sameReview(previous, attempt)` from the break) are both green, 21/21. The only update-round fixture (`updateRound()`) links round 2 to round 1 of the same key with no reviewId mismatch. Nothing builds a `previousRunId` that points at another Review, PR, repository or viewer.
- Why it matters: this gate decides whose operator answers get pre-selected in a publication draft. If it regresses, answers from a different Review can be pre-filled into a review that is about to be published, and no test notices.
- Fix: add a case where round 1 and round 2 have different `reviewId`s (or round 1 is re-keyed to another viewer/number) and round 1 has a saved draft. Assert `preview.settings.answers` is `{}` for round 2.

### [F-003] Walking past an earlier attempt without a draft is unpinned
- Severity: minor
- File: src/review/desk-publication.ts:56 (`answerSourceLimit`), 150-156 (`earlierAnswers`)
- Problem: M06 (limit 8 -> 1) and M19 (return from the first directory even when it has no draft) are both green. Both answer-carry tests use a two-attempt chain where the immediate predecessor holds the draft. No fixture has an intermediate attempt with no `publication-draft.json`. The documented behaviour, "the newest earlier attempt that saved a publication draft", is therefore never exercised beyond depth one.
- Why it matters: an update round after an unpublished intermediate round (the common case for repeated pushes) would lose the operator's answers if either gate regressed, and nothing would fail.
- Fix: build a three-attempt chain: round 1 with a draft, round 2 with none, round 3 previewed. Assert round 3 gets round 1's answers. Optionally add a chain longer than `answerSourceLimit` that asserts nothing carries.

### [F-004] The executor's closed-package hand-on branch is unpinned
- Severity: minor
- File: src/review/desk-run-executor.ts:595 (`manifest.status === "closed" ||`)
- Problem: M22 (`manifest.status === "closed"` -> `false`) leaves desk-run-executor.test.ts green, 40/40. The new `previousAttempt()` helper accepts `status`, but no test passes `"closed"`. The two AC7 tests cover the partial path and its refusals only. This diff rewrote the condition (`usable && (closed || partial-record)`).
- Why it matters: handing on a completed round's closed package is the normal case for an update round. If it regresses, every update round after a completed round becomes a full review, and the suite stays green.
- Fix: add `runWithPrevious(previousAttempt({ status: "closed", findings: [priorFinding] }), false, …)`. Assert the package is copied into the checkout and that no `desk-partial` registry entry is written.
