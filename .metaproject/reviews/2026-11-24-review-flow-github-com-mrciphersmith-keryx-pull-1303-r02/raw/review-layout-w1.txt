## Findings

### [LY-001] With Update review offered, the publish footer wraps at the 720px minimum window and leaves "Publish as is for …" alone on a second row (unmeasured)

- **Severity**: info (unverified, no measurement)
- **File**: apps/desktop/src/review/ReviewPublishDialog.tsx
- **Problem**: In the head-moved, unpublished, not-skipped state, the footer holds five nowrap buttons: Update review (primary, first), Close, Skip publication, Save locally and Publish as is. That totals about 696px, against about 624px of footer content width at the 720px window. Flex-wrap moves Publish as is alone to row 2. At text-scale "larger" (1.22) the footer wraps even at the full 780px dialog width.
- **Measurement**: none. These are token-based estimates (see the table above). Settle it with a Playwright or real-CSS probe that renders ReviewPublishDialog with `headMoved` and `onUpdateReview` at window widths 720, 792 and 1180 and text-scale 1 and 1.22, reading `getBoundingClientRect().top` per footer button.
- **Why it matters**: The two mutually exclusive head-moved actions appear on different rows, and the primary is at the leading edge rather than the trailing one. Nothing is clipped and there is no sideways scroll, so this is cosmetic grouping only.
- **Fix**: Group the head-moved pair in its own nowrap flex container so they wrap together, or place the primary last in the footer. Then measure at 720px.
