### Looked at and not a finding (Iron Laws 1 and 2: no named input that reaches a cost)
- `recordHostQuestion` runs once per operator act, by hand or by an agent after a question. Per call it does: one `estimatePlan` (confirm-plan only, the same call the terminal path makes), one `findHeldFinding` or `operatorDecisionSubject`, one sync `readRunProfile`, one package-file rewrite (same as the terminal path), then `openDecision` and `answerDecision`. - Journal cost: `openDecision` takes the journal lock and reads the whole journal once (`readRecords` in `openLocked`), `answerDecision` reads it once more. That is O(journal lines) twice per act, in one parse pass each. Even at thousands of lines this is milliseconds against a CLI that costs 1.4 s to start. The journal is append-only with no pruning, but the growth source is the existing decisions feature; this diff adds one open and one answer per operator act.
- Journal lock timeout: a stale lock held by a live reused pid makes `openDecision` wait up to 15 s (`JOURNAL_LOCK_TIMEOUT_MS`) and then throw; the diff catches it and logs a warning after the review record is already written. It needs a lock younger than 60 s whose pid was reused by a live process; no reproducible path in this code, so not reported.
- Unbounded verbatim text: `answerText` and `questionText` go into `operator-decisions.json` and `plan-confirmation.json` uncapped (the journal copy is capped by `storedOperatorText` at 2000 chars and `oneLine` at 300 chars). The only producer is one command-line argument, which the OS caps near 128 KB; reading and showing it costs microseconds. Observation only.
