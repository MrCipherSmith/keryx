## Security review, slice-02 (PR #1124, round 1)

No blockers, majors or minors. One info observation, not published as a finding.

**The 250 ms clock lead does not widen what a grant can do.** The lead moves only the lower bound on the issue time. Three bounds remain independent of it: the absolute expiry (`expires > now`), the grant length (`expires - issued <= 5000` plus the new `expires > issued`), and the monotonic request age (`elapsed < 5000` ms, measured from before the authorize request and re-checked on every `apply()`). The last one caps the total usable lifetime whatever issue time the Hub states, so the worst-case wall expiry of `now + 5250 ms` is never usable.

**Attacker who controls the issuing clock.** The issuer is the Hub, which already writes the grant's whole content. Setting `authorizedAt` 250 ms ahead gains nothing over setting it to now. The lead cannot pre-mint a grant for a later request, because `startedAt` precedes the request and the grant is single-use. Replay of a stored grant is unchanged: its issue time is in the past, and the Hub-side replay path is pre-existing.

**Is the window reachable other than by skew.** The lead is reached by Hub-ahead clock skew, a wall-clock step, or a sleep that wakes marginally early. `authorize()` already sleeps out a lead of up to 500 ms before verification (commit 565866111), so the 250 ms tolerance is the residual slack for that sleep, not a new input. Every consumer goes through `MachineConnection.authorization()`; the class has no other site.

**The new `expires > issued` guard is needed and correct.** Without it, a grant with `expires <= issued` and `expires > now` would pass once the lead exists. The tests pin both the 250/251 ms edge and the inversion cases.

**Checked and clean:** injection, XSS (no `dangerouslySetInnerHTML`/`innerHTML`/`eval` in the slice), secrets and credentials (the only `token`/`secret` hits are i18n strings and CSS token names), storage, open redirect. The slice's other 17 files are tests, i18n and CSS.

**Local test failures.** The two failures in the supplied run (`R4.4` bridge timeout, `client.test.ts:134` connect error) are not claimed: neither touches the grant path, and CI is green at this head.
