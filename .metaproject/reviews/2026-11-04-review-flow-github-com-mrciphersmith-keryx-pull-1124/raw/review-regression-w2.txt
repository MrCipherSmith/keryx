## Summary

No regression found in slice-02 (18 files). No finding is filed.

**The changed grant check (`src/lib/hub-machine/client.ts`).** `verifyAuthorizationGrant` now accepts an issue time up to `MAX_HUB_CLOCK_LEAD_MS = 250` ahead of the machine clock. It also adds `expires > issued`. Callers, enumerated with `keryx ctx rg "verifyAuthorizationGrant"` over `src/`, `scripts/` and `apps/`:
- `MachineConnection.authorize` (client.ts:209). It sleeps up to 500 ms to `authorizedAt` before verifying. Grants that were valid before are still valid, and a lead above 250 ms is still refused as before.
- `createGrantApplication().valid` (client.ts:296). This is what `src/lib/hub-machine/bridge.ts` reaches through `application.valid()` and `application.apply()` at the queued, controller and selection authority checks (bridge.ts:1126 and 1131 and the apply sites at 752 and 760). `src/lib/hub-machine/admission.ts` `applyStartingCommand` also uses it, as does `scripts/verify-native-coverage.ts` through `MachineConnection`. The callers still refuse the same expired, over-limit, wrong-digest, wrong-target and wrong-controller grants. The expiry and monotonic age bounds are unchanged.

The only inputs whose result moves are grants issued 1 to 250 ms ahead of the machine clock. Those were refused before and are accepted now, which is the intent of the change. The new `expires > issued` clause cannot reject anything the old clause accepted. Under the old `issued <= now` and `expires > now`, expiry was already after issue. The Hub issues grants as `authorizedAt = t` and `expiresAt = t + LIMITS.grantMs` from one clock read (`src/hub/remote-v2/index.ts:349`). So `expires - issued <= grantMs` holds with equality, and no existing producer is newly refused. The existing grant tests in `client.test.ts` and `admission.test.ts` keep their meaning: they use `authorizedAt = now` or earlier, or a fake monotonic clock with a fixed wall clock.
