## Summary

Nothing was reviewed in a live engine: no render, no screenshots, no computed contrast or measured target sizes. Everything below comes from reading the diff, the CSS rules it depends on (the rail collapse is scoped to 1024px and up), the workspace tabs and the remote/workflow locale files that the slice's keys resolve against.

No blocker or major. The new states hold up: the session-launch blocker line names the first unmet condition in order, the rejected-opening notice hands focus to the form, the slash and Escape handling is explicit and explained in the hint, and every new i18n key exists in both EN and RU.

What is left is mostly cost on the user:

- **Recovery path loses the launch values (UX-001).** After a rejected opening, 'Create a new session' opens a blank form because success cleared the launch draft; the first message survives only in the dead session's composer.
- **History hides what it concerns (UX-002).** Only Flow and document entities resolve to names; proposal, revision and delivery rows show no subject, and other actors' raw ids are still printed.
- **EN naming drift (UX-003).** The new button says 'Project work'; the nav and the page say 'Managed work'. RU is consistent.
- **Copy and states (UX-004, UX-005, UX-006, UX-008, UX-009, UX-010).** Bare CTA labels 'All' and 'Documents', an untranslated availability token and a silently disabled Invoke, raw error codes and JSON in the transcript, a collapsing work-context summary that hides the mode, a launch Flow field that does not explain itself, and RU terminology drift for evidence.
- **Collapsed rail (UX-007).** Projects with the same initial are indistinguishable for keyboard users, who get no hover tooltip.

Three `info` observations (toggle ARIA doubling, a standing explainer notice on the Flows page, catalogue interaction depth) need a live pass or a screen-reader check to settle.
